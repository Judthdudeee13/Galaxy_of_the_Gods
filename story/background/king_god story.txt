*The oldest records speak not of kings... nor kingdoms... but of the one who ruled above them all.*

*The First God.*

*The King of Gods.*

*Forged not by flesh, nor by stone, but by the strength of countless warriors.*

*His body is iron.*

*His blood is molten steel.*

*His heart is an eternal flame.*

*Long ago, the gods sealed their king beneath the earth, fearing that no world could endure his power.*

*The seal shall remain unbroken...*

*...until the strength of a kingdom is offered to the fire.*

*Gather the weapons of its armies.*

*Cast them into a single blazing forge.*

*Let steel become liquid...*

*Let countless blades become one.*

*When the final weapon is consumed...*

*The flame shall become flesh.*

*The King of Gods shall rise once more.*

*And no throne forged by mortals shall stand before him.*
